# Lull App Audience Brief

**Date:** May 19, 2026  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Purpose:** Product, marketing, growth, and positioning decisions

## 1. Core Audience Profile

**“Wired-but-tired brains”** — ambitious, analytical overthinkers who biohack everything else in life but still struggle with sleep.

- Founders, startup execs & high-performers  
- ADHDers / neurodivergents  
- Shift workers  
- New moms/parents  
- Anyone whose mind stays in “problem-solving mode” long after their body is physically ready for sleep  

They are self-aware, data-oriented, and have already spent money on wearables, supplements, and protocols because generic advice has repeatedly failed them.

## 2. Key Pain Points

- **The disconnect**: Body is tired and ready, but the brain **won’t shut off** — racing thoughts, tomorrow’s to-do list, 3 a.m. doomscrolling spirals.  
- **Guesswork fatigue**: They’ve tried every checklist, meditation app, tracker, and white-noise solution, yet still don’t know *which* variable actually works for *their* unique brain/body.  
- **Decision overload at night**: Willpower and decision fatigue make execution impossible even when they know screens, caffeine, or rumination are the culprits.  
- **Lack of ownership**: Wearables give pretty graphs but no repeatable “test this one thing for 5 nights and here’s the verdict” loop.

## 3. Behavioral Patterns

- **Experimental mindset**: They already run personal tests and obsess over metrics (Oura, Whoop, Apple Watch, etc.).  
- **High-intent, low-tolerance**: They research deeply and will complete a structured 5-night experiment if it feels like a real lab, not another generic habit checklist.  
- **Share when it works**: They love posting before/after results, graphs, and “here’s what finally fixed my wired brain” stories.  
- **Nighttime vulnerability**: Often scrolling TikTok/IG/X at 10–11 p.m. looking for the next hack — exactly when Lull’s ritual + in-app blocking needs to intercept them.  
- **Willing to pay for results**: Proven by existing founder pre-orders; they’ll invest in something that delivers personalized, ownable proof.

## 5. Brands They Deeply Resonate With

These brands feel like part of their identity:
- **Oura Ring** — data they trust  
- **Eight Sleep** — passive environment optimization  
- **Whoop** — performance/recovery tracking  
- **Apollo Neuro** — nervous-system calm for wired brains  
- **Bulletproof / Dave Asprey ecosystem** — foundational biohacking  
- **Thesis nootropics** — personalized experimentation  

## Positioning Takeaway for Lull

**Lull is the missing behavioral layer** that turns their expensive hardware and protocols into actual, testable, ownable results.  

One-variable experiments + in-app blocking + optional device correlations = the personal sleep lab they’ve been looking for.
