Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how TenThirty ("TenThirty," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, discloses, and protects information when you use our mobile application, websites, subscriptions, paywalls, and related services (collectively, the "Service").

TenThirty is designed to be local-first where practical. Some information stays on your device. Some limited information is sent to service providers so we can operate subscriptions, understand product usage, improve the app, and maintain the Service.

1. Important Health and Safety Note

TenThirty is a general wellness and sleep routine app. It is not a medical device, health care provider, clinical service, diagnosis tool, treatment, therapy, or substitute for professional medical advice. Do not use TenThirty for emergencies or to make medical decisions.

Although sleep-related information can be sensitive, TenThirty is not intended to create, receive, maintain, or transmit protected health information on behalf of a covered entity or business associate under HIPAA.

2. Information You Provide or Create

Depending on how you use the Service, you may provide or create information such as:

  • your first name or display name inside the app;
  • onboarding answers about sleep problems, wake factors, routines, bedtime, wake time, and sleep preferences;
  • sleep logs, subjective sleep scores, notes, sleep duration, morning check-ins, routine completion, and experiment history;
  • routine steps, settings, app-blocking selections, reminders, and preferences;
  • voice recordings or voice notes if you choose to use recording features;
  • support requests or messages you send to us.

3. Information Collected Automatically

We may collect or generate certain technical and usage information, including:

  • a random per-install identifier;
  • app version, build number, platform, general app events, feature usage, and subscription tier;
  • routine step events, completion status, duration buckets, sleep score buckets, chronotype or sleep pattern categories, and other product analytics;
  • purchase and entitlement status from Apple and RevenueCat;
  • diagnostic information such as network errors or failed requests.

We do not use this information to track you across apps or websites for third-party advertising.

4. Camera, Microphone, Audio, Notifications, and App Blocking

Camera

If you allow camera access, TenThirty may briefly use the camera to estimate ambient light level during wind-down. The app reads exposure-related sensor values to derive a light-level bucket. TenThirty does not display, save, upload, or record camera images or video for this feature.

Microphone and Recordings

If you allow microphone access and choose to record a brain dump or voice note, the recording is saved locally on your device. TenThirty's product analytics may record that a recording exists and its approximate duration, but the audio file itself is not uploaded by the app's standard recording flow.

Audio

TenThirty may play sleep sounds, breathing audio, or other audio content. We may collect usage events such as whether an audio session started, completed, or how long it played.

Notifications and Live Activities

If enabled, TenThirty uses notifications and Live Activities to remind you about routines, check-ins, and sleep-related actions. You can manage notification permissions in iOS settings.

App Blocking

If you use app-blocking features, TenThirty uses Apple's Family Controls and Managed Settings frameworks to apply your selections on device. Your blocked app/category selections are stored for the app's functionality and may be synced between the app and its extension through Apple's app group storage.

5. Research and Analytics Data

TenThirty may send limited event data to analytics and research infrastructure so we can understand whether the app is working, improve routines, debug features, and evaluate aggregate sleep-pattern insights. This may include sleep scores, hours slept, routine steps, step attempts, light-level buckets, perceived temperature, media-session metadata, onboarding categories, and a random install identifier.

We do not intentionally send raw voice recordings, camera images, video, or free-text journal contents to analytics or research systems in the normal app flow. Avoid entering information that you do not want processed as described in this policy.

6. Purchases and Subscriptions

Purchases are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to manage subscription status, products, offerings, paywalls, restore purchases, and entitlements. We receive information from Apple and RevenueCat such as product identifiers, entitlement status, transaction state, renewal state, and related purchase metadata. We do not receive your full payment card number from Apple.

  • store your preferences, sleep logs, notes, and progress;
