# TenThirty Sleep-Contract Pivot

## Premise

TenThirty is a sleep commitment app: choose the rules, and TenThirty enforces the timing.

Primary promise: **No scroll when it matters.**

Primary marketing hook: **Stop the bedtime scroll before it steals tomorrow.**

## Core Mechanics

- Users choose 1-3 sleep rules: morning sun, caffeine cutoff, workout cutoff, warm shower/bath, dim lights, tomorrow's plan, gratitude journal.
- Each rule has a due time and default grace window. Caffeine and workout due times are dynamic from bedtime.
- If a rule misses its grace window, selected distraction apps lock.
- Users recover inside TenThirty with a hold-to-confirm action. Late recovery starts a 10-minute cooldown before apps can unlock.
- During the sleep window, selected distraction apps are locked no matter what.
- TenThirty itself must remain available so users can complete rules and recover.

## Firefly Criteria

One firefly is earned per night only when:

- all selected rules are completed within their grace windows,
- sleep-window lock was active,
- no emergency bypass was used during the sleep window.

Trying to open a blocked app does not lose the firefly if the shield stops the attempt.

## Marketing

Use:

- "Block bedtime scrolling"
- "Pick the sleep rules your future self wants."
- "TenThirty locks your distractions when timing matters."
- "I blocked TikTok at bedtime for 7 nights."
- "This app locks Instagram when I miss my own sleep rules."

Avoid leading with:

- personalized routine,
- meditation,
- better sleep guarantees,
- earning fireflies as the main motivation.
